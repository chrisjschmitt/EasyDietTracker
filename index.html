Welcome to EasyDiet!

📊 Track Your Day

The calorie ring and progress bars show your daily intake. Tap any bar to see which foods contributed.

🍎 Log Food by Category

Tap a food icon to add 1 serving.
Long-press to enter a specific amount.

🔍 Search Specific Foods

Tap the search icon to find foods from our database of 200+ items with detailed nutrition info.

ℹ️ Get More Info

Tap the ℹ button on any food or tracker to learn more about it.

🔄 Reset Your Day

Tap the reset button to clear today's servings.
Long-press to clear all data and refresh the app.

⚙️ Customize Targets

Tap the gear icon to adjust your calorie and macro targets.
